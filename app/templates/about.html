About the Prefa.News

The Prefa.News is a digital platform created to aggregate, analyze and amplify news and positive initiatives of the public power in municipalities of Santa Catarina.

Every day, municipalities develop valuable projects and high impact — but these stories often remain restricted to their city of origin. The Prefa.News connects municipal portals, catalogs and amplifies access to these news, so that citizens know what happens near them and public managers can be inspired by solutions already tested in other municipalities.
